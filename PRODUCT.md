# Product

## Register

product

## Users

HelpShop serves elderly shoppers and their children or family caregivers. Elderly users need a clear, readable shopping flow for everyday goods. Child users need to help bind an elderly account, manage addresses, view reminders, and assist with shopping decisions.

## Product Purpose

HelpShop is a HarmonyOS shopping assistance app. Its immediate goal is a local demo loop: unified login and registration, identity certification and family binding, elderly shopping, child-assisted shopping, order history, address management, and reminder records.

## Brand Personality

Warm, trustworthy, restrained. The interface should feel like a mature commerce app rather than a prototype, while staying friendly to elderly users.

## Anti-references

Avoid cheap saturated orange/yellow styling, crowded decorative cards, landing-page layouts, playful gradients, and direct unauthenticated switching between elderly and child modes. The app should not feel like a template or a simple class demo.

## Design Principles

Use familiar commerce navigation so users can orient quickly.
Keep primary flows readable with generous touch targets and clear states.
Use one restrained warm palette across all pages.
Separate elderly and child responsibilities through account identity and binding.
Prefer realistic product UI over decorative marketing sections.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Prioritize high contrast, large readable text, clear button labels, and stable layouts for elderly users. Keep motion minimal and use color as a supporting signal rather than the only status indicator.
